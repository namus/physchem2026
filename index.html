About The Meeting

A National Flagship Gathering of Physical Chemists

Bringing together experimental and theoretical physical and biophysical chemists from across India to cross-fertilize ideas, discuss new research paradigms, and foster interdisciplinary collaborations.

Exploring Frontiers Across Experiments, Computations & Theory

The Frontiers of Physical Chemistry 2026 conference is conceived as a premier national platform to showcase the vibrant landscape of physical and biophysical chemistry research in India.

Over three and a half intensive days (October 28–31, 2026), the meeting will host keynote and invited lectures by leading pioneers, presentations by promising young faculty members and postdoctoral researchers, as well as extensive poster sessions.

A unique hallmark and signature tradition of these Physical Chemistry symposia has been active poster presentations by senior faculty members and mentors alongside enthusiastic PhD scholars and students.

Scientific Scope

Core Conference Themes

Spanning fundamental theory, quantum dynamics, state-of-the-art spectroscopic experiments, and modern computational methodologies at the interface of chemistry, physics, and biology.

AI & Machine Learning in Chemistry

Neural network potential energy surfaces, generative molecular discovery, data-driven kinetics, and AI-accelerated quantum mechanics.

Molecular Dynamics of Complex Liquids

Hydration shells, ionic liquids, deep eutectic solvents, glass transition dynamics, and solvation phenomena across time scales.

Ultrafast & Nonlinear Spectroscopy

Femtosecond transient absorption, 2D-IR, THz time-domain spectroscopy, single-molecule fluorescence, and cavity ring-down methods.

Chemical Physics & Quantum Chemistry

Electronic structure theory, coupled-cluster methods, DFT, relativistic quantum chemistry, non-adiabatic transitions, and quantum materials.

Chemical Reaction Dynamics & Kinetics

Molecular beam scattering, transition state theory, tunneling kinetics, gas-phase & interfacial reactions, and catalytic mechanism mapping.

Electrochemistry & Energy Materials

Interfacial charge transfer, battery & fuel cell chemistry, porous organic frameworks (POFs/MOFs), electrocatalysis, and solar energy conversion.

Atmospheric & Astrochemistry

Aerosol chemistry, trace gas monitoring, interstellar dust & icy grain reactions, planetary atmospheres, and environmental photochemistry.

Method Development: Experiments & Computations

Enhanced free energy sampling methods, QM/MM algorithms, advanced optical instrumentation, and multiscale modeling approaches.

About the Host Institution (SNBNCBS)

Established in 1986 under the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, S. N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences is an autonomous research institute dedicated to honoring the legacy of legendary Indian theoretical physicist Professor Satyendra Nath Bose.

The centre boasts modern auditorium facilities (Silver Jubilee Hall), on-campus guest houses, scientific computing facilities, and state-of-the-art physical chemistry research laboratories.

First Announcement

Frontiers of Physical Chemistry 2026

Experiments, Computations and Theory

A flagship meeting of Physical Chemistry in India

October 28–31, 2026 | S. N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences, Kolkata

Dear Colleagues,

We are pleased to announce the upcoming national conference "Frontiers of Physical Chemistry: Experiments, Computations and Theory" — a flagship meeting of Physical Chemistry in India — to be held at the S. N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences (SNBNCBS), Kolkata, from October 28–31, 2026 (Wednesday–Saturday).

This conference aims to bring together experimental and theoretical/computational physical and biophysical chemists from across India under one umbrella, to cross-fertilise ideas, share new research findings, and foster collaborative research. The scientific programme will span the frontiers of physical and biophysical chemistry, including AI/ML in chemistry, molecular dynamics of complex liquids and biomolecules, ultrafast spectroscopy, chemical reaction dynamics, electrochemistry, atmospheric chemistry, astrochemistry, and other interdisciplinary areas at the interface of chemistry, physics, and biology.

The meeting will feature an outstanding line-up of invited speakers from leading institutions across the country, along with poster sessions and short presentations by young faculty members, postdoctoral researchers, and students. Poster presentations by senior faculty members have been a signature of these Physical Chemistry conferences.

This is a first announcement, and we request you to kindly mark your calendars for these dates. Detailed information regarding registration, abstract submission, accommodation, and the full scientific programme will be circulated in due course.

We look forward to welcoming you to Kolkata.
